Preferred & Non-Preferred Foods Matrix

At Himalayan altitudes (2,000m to 5,300m), low oxygen concentration slows gastric peristalsis by 35% while icy glacial streams carry latent pathogen cysts. The Department has audited all regional foods across 4 critical scientific criteria.

🔬

Criterion 1: Thermal Sterilization

Food must be freshly cooked or steamed above 75°C (167°F). Raw or ambient foods carry extreme Giardia and Amoebic cyst risks in mountain aquifers.

🫁

Criterion 2: Altitude Gastric Motility

Oxygen is prioritized to heart and brain. Easily hydrolyzed complex carbs digest smoothly; heavy animal saturated fats cause severe nausea, acid reflux, and exacerbate AMS.

💧

Criterion 3: Glacial Water & Ice Safety

Non-RO ice cubes and tap water carry high bacterial loads from upstream grazing. Strictly consume boiled herbal infusions or multi-stage filtered water.

❄️

Criterion 4: Cold-Chain Integrity

Unstable mountain highway power causes repeated thawing and refreezing of meat. Fresh daily river/farm produce is preferred over stored highway meats.
